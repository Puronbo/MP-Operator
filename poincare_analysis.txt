### entropy_monotonicity
**Zero-condition interpretation**: The 'zero' corresponds to vanishing entropy production. Perelman's entropy formula W(g,f,τ) = ∫[τ(|∇f|² + R) + f - n](4πτ)^{-n/2}e^{-f}dV has derivative dW/dt ≥ 0, with equality only for gradient shrinking solitons. The 'zero' condition is when the flow is a gradient shrinking soliton (dW/dt = 0), and otherwise dW/dt > 0 (entropy strictly increasing).
- State space: Space of Riemannian metrics with potential function f and scale parameter τ
- Evolution equation: Coupled system ∂g/∂t = -2Ric(g) + 2∇²f, ∂f/∂t = -Δf - R + n/(2τ), ∂τ/∂t = -1
- Concrete estimate: Prove dW/dt = 2τ∫|Ric + ∇²f - (1/(2τ))g|²(4πτ)^{-n/2}e^{-f}dV ≥ 0

### reduced_volume_noncollapsing
**Zero-condition interpretation**: The 'zero' corresponds to vanishing volume ratio. Perelman's reduced volume Ṽ(τ) = ∫(4πτ)^{-n/2}e^{-l}dV where l is reduced length. The non-collapsing theorem says if Ṽ(τ₀) > δ > 0 at some time, then Ṽ(τ) ≥ δ' > 0 for all later times (with δ' depending on δ). The 'zero' condition is when the volume ratio collapses to zero (Ṽ → 0), indicating formation of singularities with collapsed limits.
- State space: Space of Riemannian metrics under Ricci flow with surgery, tracking volume ratios
- Evolution equation: Reduced length l satisfies ∂l/∂t = -Δl + R + |∇l|², and Ṽ(τ) = (4πτ)^{-n/2}∫e^{-l}dV
- Concrete estimate: Prove lower bound on Ṽ(τ) in terms of Ṽ(τ₀) using the monotonicity formula and curvature estimates
